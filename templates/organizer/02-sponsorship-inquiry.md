**Subject:** Sponsorship opportunities for [Event Name] 2027?

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

Our team at [Your Company] attended [Event Name] this year, and we were incredibly impressed by the [Audience Quality/Foot Traffic/Vibe].

We provide [Brief description of what you do, e.g., an AI-native event intelligence platform] to [Target Audience], and it feels like a perfect match for the attendees at [Event Name]. 

Could you send over the sponsorship prospectus for next year? We’re specifically interested in [Type of Sponsorship, e.g., a 10x10 booth or a VIP lounge sponsorship] and would love to lock something in early.

**💡 When to use this:**
When you want to evaluate exhibiting or sponsoring a trade show. Organizers love this email and will often give you early-bird pricing or better booth locations if you reach out proactively.
