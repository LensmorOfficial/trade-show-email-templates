# Post-Show: The Breakup Email

**Subject:** Closing the loop / Time to cross {{Company}} off my list?

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

I haven't heard back from you since [Event Name], so I'm guessing this isn't a priority for {{Company}} right now or you're swamped catching up post-show.

Either way, I don't want to keep cluttering your inbox. If you’re still interested in how we help with [Specific Problem], let me know. Otherwise, I’ll assume the timing isn't right and won't reach out again.

Wishing you a great rest of the year!

Best regards,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When you've sent 3-4 follow-ups with no response. The "breakup" email is famously effective because it leverages FOMO (fear of missing out) and simple human nature. It gives them an easy "out" or prompts a "sorry, I've been busy, yes let's talk!" response.
