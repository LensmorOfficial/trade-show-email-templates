# Post-Show: Competitor Mention

> **When to use:** You spotted a prospect using a specific tool or process at their booth that your product can replace or improve. Shows you paid attention to their setup.
> **Best timing:** Within 72 hours after the event
> **Expected response rate:** 15-25%

## Subject Line Options

1. `[First Name] — spotted your setup at [Show Name]`
2. `[Show Name] — quick thought on [Competitor Tool]`
3. `[Company] booth at [Show Name] — one observation`

## Email Body

Hi [First Name],

I noticed at [Show Name] that your team was using [Competitor Tool / current process, e.g., manual badge scanners].

A lot of companies we work with ran into the same issues — [Pain Point, e.g., leads falling through the cracks with no context attached]. They moved to [Your Product] because it [Key Benefit, e.g., automatically enriches and scores leads in real time].

Since you're already investing in trade shows, would a 10-minute comparison be useful?

Best,
[Your Name]

## Variables

| Variable | Description | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| [First Name] | Recipient's first name | Sophie |
| [Company] | Their company name | VendorCo |
| [Show Name] | Trade show name | SaaStr Annual |
| [Competitor Tool] | The tool or method you observed | a manual badge scanner |
| [Pain Point] | Common problem with that approach | leads with no follow-up context |
| [Your Product] | Your product name | Lensmor |
| [Key Benefit] | What you do better | enriches and scores leads instantly |
| [Your Name] | Your full name | Alex Chen |

## Tips

- Be specific — generic "I noticed you use a competitor" emails don't land; name what you actually saw
- Stay observational, not condescending — you're offering a solution, not criticizing their choices
- If you're not sure what they're using, ask a question instead: "What are you using for lead capture this season?"
- Follow up with a short comparison doc or 2-minute Loom video showing the difference
