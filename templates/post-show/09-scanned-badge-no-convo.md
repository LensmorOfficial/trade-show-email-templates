# Post-Show: Scanned Badge, No Conversation

> **When to use:** You have a lead in your scanner but no notes — they may have grabbed a pen, entered a raffle, or breezed past the booth. Acknowledge the lack of context and offer immediate value.
> **Best timing:** Within 48 hours after the event
> **Expected response rate:** 8-15%

## Subject Line Options

1. `Sorry we didn't connect properly at [Show Name]`
2. `Quick note from the [Your Company] booth`
3. `[Show Name] — we crossed paths`

## Email Body

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for stopping by our booth at [Show Name]. It looks like we scanned your badge but didn't get a chance to actually talk — the floor was busy.

I wanted to follow up because a lot of [their role, e.g., Marketing Directors] who came by were asking about [Key Problem You Solve]. Here's a [one-pager / short video] that explains how we approach it: [Link]

If it's relevant, I'd love to set up a quick 10-minute call. No pressure either way.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Title], [Your Company]

## Variables

| Variable | Description | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| [First Name] | Recipient's first name | Chris |
| [Your Company] | Your company name | Lensmor |
| [Show Name] | Trade show name | GITEX Global |
| [their role] | Their job title | Sales Directors |
| [Key Problem You Solve] | Core pain you address | finding high-intent exhibitors before the show |
| [one-pager / video] | Resource type | 2-minute product overview |
| [Link] | URL to the resource | https://www.lensmor.com/demo |
| [Your Name] | Your full name | Alex Chen |
| [Your Title] | Your job title | Head of Partnerships |

## Tips

- Acknowledge the lack of context directly — it feels honest and stands out
- Lead with value (a resource) before asking for a call
- Keep the ask minimal: 10 minutes is less threatening than "a demo"
- If you have any signal from their badge (company, title), personalize accordingly
- Don't pretend you had a full conversation if you didn't — prospects will remember
