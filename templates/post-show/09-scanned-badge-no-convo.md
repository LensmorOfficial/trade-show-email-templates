# Post-Show: Scanned Badge but No Conversation

**Subject:** Sorry we missed a real chat at [Event Name] / Thanks for stopping by!

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

Thanks for dropping by our booth at [Event Name]. I see we scanned your badge, but it looks like we didn’t get a chance to have a proper conversation about {{Company}}. It was incredibly busy on the floor!

I wanted to quickly follow up because many [Title/Role, e.g., Marketing Directors] who visited us were specifically interested in how we [Key Benefit].

Here is a quick [1-pager/Video Demo] that explains what we do. If this looks relevant, are you open to a quick 10-minute introductory call next week?

Best,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When you have a lead in your scanner but no notes on what they wanted. They might have grabbed a pen, entered a raffle, or just breezed by. Acknowledge the lack of context and offer immediate value.
