# Pre-Show: Customer Invitation

**Subject:** Catch up at [Event Name]? / Coffee on us at [Event Name]

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

I noticed {{Company}} is attending/exhibiting at [Event Name] next month. 

Since you’re already a valued customer, I’d love to personally invite you to stop by our booth ([Booth Number]) for a quick hello. We’ll be showcasing [New Feature/Product] that I think your team will really benefit from.

Are you free for a quick 10-minute catch-up on [Date] or [Date]? If not, feel free to just drop by whenever you have a moment. We'll have [swag/coffee/treat] waiting for you!

Best,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When you know your existing customers are attending the show. It's a great opportunity to strengthen the relationship, upsell new features, or just show appreciation.
