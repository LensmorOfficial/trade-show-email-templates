# Pre-Show: VIP Dinner / Happy Hour Invite

**Subject:** Exclusive Dinner with [Your Company] at [Event Name]

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

Since you'll be at [Event Name] this year, I wanted to personally invite you to an exclusive VIP [Dinner/Happy Hour/Event] we are hosting on [Date] at [Time]. 

We’re bringing together a small, curated group of [industry/role, e.g., VP-level Marketing leaders] for an evening of great food, drinks, and casual networking at [Venue Name]. 

Space is limited to just [Number] guests, so please let me know by [RSVP Date] if you can make it, and I’ll send over the official calendar invite and details.

Hope to see you there!

Best regards,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When you are hosting an off-site event (dinner, party, breakfast) alongside the trade show. This is one of the highest-converting ways to build relationships with key accounts.
