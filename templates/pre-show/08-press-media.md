# Pre-Show: Press & Media Pitch

**Subject:** Story Idea: How {{Company}} is changing [Industry] at [Event Name]

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

I saw that you're covering [Event Name] this year for [Publication Name]. 

I know your readers are always interested in [Topic relevant to their beat, e.g., AI-driven sales tools]. At the show, [Your Company] will be launching [New Product/Data Report], which reveals how [Key Insight/Statistic].

I’d love to offer you an exclusive briefing or an interview with our [CEO/Founder/Expert], [Name], who will be onsite at Booth [Number]. 

Are you available for a 15-minute chat on [Date] to discuss this? I can send over a press kit in advance.

Best,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When reaching out to journalists, bloggers, or industry analysts attending the event. Focus on the story and the value to their audience, not just a product pitch.
