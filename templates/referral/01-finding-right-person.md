# Referral Email: Who Is the Right Person?

**Subject:** Implementing [Your Solution] at {{Company}} / Connecting regarding [Topic] at {{Company}}

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

I’m trying to figure out who is in charge of [related issue or department, e.g., trade show lead generation / event marketing] at {{Company}}. 

Would you mind pointing me in the right direction and letting me know the best way to get in touch with them?

Thanks so much for your help!

Best regards,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When you've identified a target company (perhaps an exhibitor at an upcoming trade show) but you don't know who handles the specific area your product/service addresses. Keep it short and make it easy for them to just reply with a name or forward it.
