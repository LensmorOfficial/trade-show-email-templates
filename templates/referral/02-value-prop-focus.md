# Referral Email: Value Proposition Focus

**Subject:** {{FirstName}}, can you point me to the right contact?

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

We recently helped [SimilarCompany/Competitor] increase their [relevant metric, e.g., trade show ROI / qualified leads] by [percentage/amount], and I believe we could do something similar for {{Company}}. 

Could you please point me to the person who handles [specific area, e.g., event strategy / field marketing] so I can share more details?

I appreciate your help! Looking forward to your response.

Best regards,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When you have a strong case study or name-drop that will immediately grab attention. Mentioning a competitor or a well-known company in their space provides instant credibility.
