# Referral Email: Research-Based Approach

**Subject:** Researching {{Company}}, who should I speak with?

**Body:**

Hi {{FirstName}},

I’ve been doing some research on {{Company}} ahead of [Upcoming Trade Show Name] and believe our [product/service] could be a great fit to help address [specific challenge, e.g., capturing and enriching leads at the booth]. 

Could you help me by pointing me to the best person to discuss this with?

Your guidance would be much appreciated!

Best,
{{YourName}}

---
**💡 When to use this:**
When you've done specific research on a company's upcoming event presence or recent initiatives. It shows you aren't just sending a mass blast, but have specifically identified them as a good fit.
